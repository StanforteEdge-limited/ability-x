# AbilityX 2.0 — Content & Intent Brief (for Claude Design)

**Context:** AbilityX is Nigeria's leading disability inclusion conference, powered by Project Enable Africa in partnership with Jobberman Nigeria. AbilityX 1.0 ran December 2, 2025, in Lagos. AbilityX 2.0 is confirmed for this **November** — exact date, venue, and theme still pending.

**This brief covers copy and content structure only.** Visual system (color, type, layout, componentry) is left to Claude Design's own design system — no existing brand constraints are being imposed here beyond the general tone below.

**Tone:** Confident, momentum-driven, credible — not a first-timer's pitch. AbilityX 1.0 already happened and delivered real numbers; 2.0 copy should read like "the movement is already proven, join what's next," not "please come to our first event."

---

## The narrative arc (applies across the whole site)

1. **What AbilityX is** — the movement, not just an event
2. **What 1.0 proved** — real numbers, real people, real momentum
3. **What 2.0 is** — building on that, this November
4. **The ask** — partnership and sponsorship first (time-sensitive), attendance second (waitlist, since logistics aren't locked)

Every page should orient somewhere on this arc. Pages don't need to repeat the full arc, but none should contradict it (e.g., nothing should read like AbilityX is unproven or speculative).

### Home — What to expect (pillars)
**Job:** Signal substance and structure without needing full 2.0 program details yet. These four pillars are theme-agnostic and proven from 1.0.

1. **Inclusive Future Dialogue** — High-level plenary convening Africa's most influential leaders to explore AI, data-driven policy, and inclusive digital economies.
2. **Innovation Labs** — Interactive breakout sessions exploring the future of work, assistive technology, the data revolution, and policy innovation.
3. **Research & Evidence Showcase** — Groundbreaking research on disability inclusion across health, finance, and key sectors in Africa.
4. **AbilityX Impact Awards** — Celebrating excellence in technology, innovation, and leadership advancing next-generation disability inclusion.

Intro line: `AbilityX 2.0 builds on the four pillars that made 1.0 work — now with a full year of momentum behind them.`

### Home — The ask (three-part CTA)
**Job:** Direct different visitor types to the right next step. Partnership is the priority ask right now (time-sensitive, sponsors need lead time); attendance is secondary (waitlist only, since dates aren't locked).

1. **Partner With Us** — `Sponsor, co-convene, or support AbilityX 2.0. Reach 450+ decision-makers, innovators, and changemakers across Africa's disability inclusion ecosystem.` → Partner inquiry
2. **Join the Waitlist** — `Registration for AbilityX 2.0 opens once dates are confirmed. Be first to know.` → Waitlist capture
3. **Exhibit Your Innovation** — `Showcase your solution to Africa's top disability inclusion stakeholders. Applications open closer to the event.` → Interest form
