# **ABILITYX 2026 WEBSITE CONTENT UPDATE**

*For implementation by the IT/Web Team*

**Context**  
Registration for AbilityX 2026 is now open and the event details have been confirmed. The homepage therefore needs to move away from the earlier pre-launch messaging and place the 2026 event, its theme and registration at the centre of the visitor experience. AbilityX 2025 content should remain on the site as proof of impact, but it should no longer take priority over the current edition.

**Registration link:** bit.ly/AbilityX2026

# **1\. Navigation**

Please update the main navigation so the current edition is clearly identified as AbilityX 2026\.

**Recommended navigation:** Home \| About \| AbilityX 2025 \| Blog \| Partner With Us \| Register

“Register” should be visually distinct from the other menu items and should link directly to bit.ly/AbilityX2026.

# **2\. Hero section**

**Current copy to remove:** “The Movement Returns This November” and “November 2026\. Lagos”.

**Replace with:**

**FROM MARGINS TO MARKETS**  
Africa’s Blueprint for Disability-Inclusive Growth

**AbilityX Disability Leadership Forum 2026**  
Thursday, November 26, 2026 \| 9:00 AM  
Paradise Event Arena, Alagomeji, Yaba, Lagos

**REGISTER FOR ABILITYX 2026  \|  PARTNER WITH US**

Registration should be the primary button. “Partner With Us” can remain as the secondary action. The current “Watch the 1.0 Recap” button should move further down the page with the 2025 content.

# **3\. The Movement / Introduction**

The existing introduction gives useful context on what AbilityX is, so the idea can remain. The second paragraph, however, still reads like the 2026 event has not been fully announced. Please replace the section with the copy below.

AbilityX is more than an event. It is Nigeria’s leading annual convening focused on the future of disability inclusion, bringing together persons with disabilities, business leaders, policymakers, innovators, development partners and civil society to shape what inclusive development can look like in Africa.

Following the first edition in 2025, AbilityX returns on November 26, 2026 with a sharper focus on economic participation. Under the theme “From Margins to Markets: Africa’s Blueprint for Disability-Inclusive Growth,” this year’s forum will examine how employment, entrepreneurship, finance, technology and policy can create real pathways to opportunity for persons with disabilities.

# **4\. Add a 2026 focus section**

This should come before the AbilityX 2025 statistics. The intention is to explain the theme before asking visitors to register.

**INCLUSION MUST LEAD TO OPPORTUNITY.**

Across Africa, persons with disabilities continue to encounter barriers to employment, entrepreneurship, finance, technology and access to markets. AbilityX 2026 will bring the people affected by these barriers into the same conversation with the institutions, businesses and leaders that can help remove them.

The focus this year is not only on being included, but on what inclusion makes possible: the ability to work, build businesses, access capital, participate in markets, innovate and create economic value.

**Focus areas:** Employment \| Entrepreneurship \| Finance \| Technology \| Policy

# **5\. Who should attend?**

Please add a short audience section so visitors can quickly see whether the forum is relevant to them.

AbilityX 2026 is for persons with disabilities, employers, entrepreneurs, policymakers, investors, development professionals, innovators, disability advocates, researchers, corporate leaders and anyone interested in building a more inclusive economy.

**If you are interested in an economy where persons with disabilities can work, build, earn, innovate and lead, you should be in the room.**

Button: Register for AbilityX 2026
